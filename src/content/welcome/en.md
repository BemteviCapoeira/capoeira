Dear friends,

This is a collection of capoeira songs, created so we can sing together and keep fuelling our training with the energy of Abadá Capoeira. EEach song includes lyrics and a link to a performance on YouTube. The songs are organized into Benguela songs and São Bento songs. The site looks best on a computer; you can also install it as an app on your phone. This is an early version; from time to time we'll add more songs and improvements. We'd love your suggestions and feedback. Thanks for stopping by, and see you at the roda!
