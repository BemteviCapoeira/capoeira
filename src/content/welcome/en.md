Dear friends,

This is a collection of capoeira songs, created so we can sing together and keep fuelling our training with the energy of Capoeira ABADA. Every song has its lyrics and a link to a performance on YouTube. The songs are tagged as Benguela songs and São Bento songs. The site looks better on a computer and you can install the app on your phone. This is an early version; from time to time we'll add more songs and improvements. We'd love to hear your suggestions and feedback. Thanks for stopping by, and see you at the roda!
